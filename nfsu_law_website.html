About NFSU Law Review

The NFSU School of Law Academic Research Portal is a student-run initiative dedicated to promoting legal scholarship, research, and academic discourse within the National Forensic Sciences University community and beyond.

Our Mission

We aim to provide a platform for students, faculty, and legal professionals to publish high-quality research papers, journal articles, and newsletters that contribute to the advancement of legal education and forensic sciences.

Editorial Board

Our editorial board consists of faculty advisors and senior students who review all submissions to ensure academic rigor and quality standards are maintained.
